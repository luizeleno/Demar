Simple Enrollment Guide designed to guide the enrollment process
{: .lead}

[Download PDF (pt-BR only)]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/docs/Guia simples de matriculas_atualizado2026.pdf){: .btn .btn-primary target="_blank"}

The "Simple Enrollment Guide" was prepared to clarify, in an accessible and objective way, the most frequent questions related to the enrollment process in courses offered at the Lorena School of Engineering, University of São Paulo (EEL/USP). Because this is a very important step in the students' academic journey, this guide should be updated according to any changes in procedures, which are established by the Undergraduate Committee of EEL/USP and the Pro-Rectorate for Undergraduate Studies (PRG/USP).
