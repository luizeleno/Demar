Professor Doutor na Escola de Engenharia de Lorena da Universidade de São Paulo (EEL-USP). Desenvolve pesquisas na área de Ciência e Engenharia de Materiais, com ênfase em materiais vítreos e vitrocerâmicos, processos de cristalização ultrarrápida assistidos por campos e correntes elétricas, sinterização assistida por campo elétrico (Flash Sintering), eletrólitos sólidos para baterias de estado sólido e técnicas avançadas de caracterização. {% contentfor en %}

Assistant Professor at the University of São Paulo, Lorena School of Engineering (EEL-USP). His research focuses on Materials Science and Engineering, with emphasis on glass and glass-ceramic materials, electric field-assisted processing, ultrafast crystallization, ion exchange, solid electrolytes for solid-state batteries, and advanced structural and electrochemical characterization techniques. His work combines experimental research, scientific instrumentation, automation, and computational modeling to develop novel materials and advanced manufacturing processes. He holds a Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering from the University of São Paulo (USP), a B.Sc. in Biosystems Engineering from USP, and a Technical Diploma in Mechatronics from ETEC Paulino Botelho. He is a founding committee member of the Young Ceramists of Brazil (JCB), an initiative of the Brazilian Ceramic Society.
